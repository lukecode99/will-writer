Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

The short version. Your will and the answers you type stay on your own device. We do not have a database of people’s wills. We only ever handle your personal details when you choose to pay for Print & Post — and even then your will is streamed once to the printer and never stored by us at rest.

Who we are

SortedWill (“we”, “us”) operates sortedwill.co.uk and is the data controller for the limited personal data described below. Contact: hello@sortedwill.co.uk.

The will you create

Everything you enter while building your will is saved locally in your own browser (device storage) so you can come back to it. It is not sent to us and we cannot see it. Clearing your browser data removes it. Keep your own backup of the finished PDF.

What we handle for Print & Post

If — and only if — you use the paid Print & Post service, we process:

DataWhyWho processes it
Recipient name & UK postal addressTo address and post your printed willUs, and our print/mail partner
Your will PDFTo print the copy you asked for — streamed once, never stored by usOur print/mail partner
Email address (optional)To send your receipt and a “posted” confirmationUs / email provider
Payment detailsTo take the £14.99 paymentStripe (we never see your card number)

The companies that help us (sub-processors)

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Intelliprint — printing and posting your will.
  • Cloudflare — securely routing your order between us, Stripe and the printer.
  • Resend — sending your confirmation email (only if you provide an email).

Each handles data only to provide their part of the service.

How long we keep it

We do not store your will after it is sent to print. We keep a minimal record of an order (a payment reference and, if given, your email) for a short period so we can confirm dispatch, handle refunds and meet our accounting duties, then it is deleted. Stripe and our print partner keep their own records under their policies.

Legal basis

We process the data above to perform the contract you enter into when you buy Print & Post (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), and to meet our legal obligations such as tax record-keeping (Article 6(1)(c)).

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or erase the personal data we hold, to object to or restrict processing, and to data portability. Email hello@sortedwill.co.uk and we’ll respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

We don’t use tracking or advertising cookies. Your device storage is used only to hold the will you’re working on. Stripe may set cookies needed to process your payment securely on its own checkout page.
