Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Creating and downloading your will on SortedWill is free. The only paid part is the optional Print & Post service (£14.99), which prints your will and posts it to you. This policy explains when that £14.99 can be refunded.

When you get a full refund

  • Payment taken but nothing printed. If your payment is charged but, for a technical reason on our side, your will is never submitted to our print partner, you are refunded in full automatically.
  • We can’t fulfil your order. If we are unable to print and post your will for any reason, you get a full refund.

When a refund is not available

  • Once printing has started. A printed will is made to your personal specification. To keep the price low we send it to print immediately after your payment is confirmed. Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 the 14-day cancellation right does not apply to goods made to your specification or clearly personalised, so once printing has begun the order can’t be cancelled or refunded.
  • Mistakes in what you entered. We print exactly the document you created. We can’t refund because a name, gift or address was typed incorrectly — please check your will on the review screen before you pay. You can always correct it and order a fresh copy.
  • Royal Mail delays. Posting is by Royal Mail 1st Class. We can’t refund for postal delays outside our control, though we’ll help you chase a lost item.

Faulty or damaged print

If your printed will arrives damaged, misprinted or incomplete, contact us within 14 days of delivery with a photo and we will reprint and repost it free, or refund you — your choice. This is in addition to your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

How to ask for a refund

Email hello@sortedwill.co.uk with the email address you used at checkout and roughly when you ordered. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days (Stripe’s timing).

This policy does not affect your statutory rights as a consumer.
